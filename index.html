The game

The Hollow Mile is a mobile-first, tile-based dungeon roguelite. Pick a class, tap a glowing tile, and advance one row at a time through a three-lane dungeon. You are not free to roam.

Every step is a choice: fight, evade, take the gold, or trip an alarm that pulls the dead closer. Combat is automatic. Survival is not. Spend gold with a travelling merchant, choose how you grow, and see how far the road below will take you.

Six kinds of traveller take that road. Meet them on the Classes page.

Privacy

Last updated 24 August 2026

The Hollow Mile is made by Greyden Scott. This page covers hollowmile.com and the forthcoming iOS app. Questions: hello@hollowmile.com.

iOS waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we store the email address you submit so we can tell you when the iOS app is available. We do not use it for other marketing, and we do not sell it. Duplicate signups are ignored. Email us to be removed.

Play telemetry

When a browser run ends in death, the site may record the player level at that moment. That value is stored without accounts, cookies, IP addresses, class, distance, or other identifiers. It is used only to balance the game.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process requests as needed to serve the pages and APIs. We do not run advertising trackers or a public analytics dashboard.
