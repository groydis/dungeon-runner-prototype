The game

The Hollow Mile is a mobile-first, tile-based dungeon roguelite. Pick a class, tap a glowing tile, and advance one row at a time through a three-lane dungeon. You are not free to roam.

Every step is a choice: fight, evade, take the gold, or trip an alarm that pulls the dead closer. Combat is automatic. Survival is not. Spend gold with a travelling merchant, choose how you grow, and see how far the road below will take you.
